# Explore before committing

**Apply when:** a novel interaction or consequential product tradeoff lacks an established direction.

Build meaningfully different, inexpensive alternatives that resolve a named decision. Reuse actual UI modules, isolate variations and compare through a common switcher where useful. Recommend a direction from observed behavior, user need and project principles.

**Example:** compare an inline notice with an overlay in the same reading workspace to assess continuity, recovery and dismissal.

**Tradeoff:** settled direction and minor polish do not require an alternatives tournament. Stop when the comparison resolves the uncertainty. Preserve rejection and partial selections as legitimate feedback; do not promote an experiment into an accepted shared design automatically.
