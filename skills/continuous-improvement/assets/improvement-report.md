For each shortlisted change:

- **Evidence and cause:** concrete paths/symbols, session events, or executed results. Separate observations from interpretation; combine both playbooks' evidence for shared causes.
- **Simpler target:** proposed change and a small before/after sketch when useful.
- **Delete / migrate / retain:** obsolete implementations, abstractions, examples, exports, instructions, or process steps to remove; consumers to migrate; useful boundaries or compatibility to preserve. Explain retention when deletion would not help.
- **Expected benefit and tradeoffs:** work made easier, recurring impact, effort, dependencies, migration/ownership cost, risk, and confidence.
- **Validation:** evidence already obtained versus the focused check or bounded experiment still needed; relevant behavior/mandatory gates to preserve; recurrence prevention where practical.
