# Preserve settled decisions

**Apply when:** revising feedback, changing a shared control, or handing an accepted direction to a new agent.

Carry forward the approved units, states and constraints. Make the next delta explicit and retain surrounding context. Show affected shared consumers before generalizing a local proposal. Reuse valid authorization and approval within their recorded scope.

**Example:** a request to change citation styling keeps the accepted reader navigation and source highlighting; a necessary shared-control change includes its other consumers in the impact review.

**Tradeoff:** prior decisions can become unsuitable. Explain the new evidence and consequential deviation, then seek the changed decision rather than silently altering the target or repeatedly asking to approve unchanged work.
