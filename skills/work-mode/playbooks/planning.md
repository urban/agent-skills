# Plan a scoped outcome

**Enter when:** choosing or revising a deliverable, its boundaries, dependencies and evidence. Reuse approved requirements and existing plans; settled executable work can enter [execution](execute.md) directly.

**Read:** recovered outcome/authorization, current implementation, requirements, owning decisions and relevant prior findings. Use [skill routing](../references/skill-routing.md) for specialists at the step that needs them.

## Shape and resolve

1. Describe the smallest coherent outcome that delivers the requested value, including implementation, integration, verification and relevant feedback effort. State the concrete before/after behavior or caller contract, exclusions, owners and affected consumers. Separate effort from uncertainty; do not omit consequential recovery just to make a slice smaller.
2. Identify what is real now, becomes real, or remains simulated, and the existing seams. Reuse evidence first. Investigate uncertainty that could invalidate the outcome through a bounded source question or [experiment](bounded-prototyping.md); return observations and limits to this plan before committing dependent work. Architecture and specification decisions use their own available expertise rather than becoming UI prerequisites.
3. If scope depends on an interaction or visual decision, discover/read `interface-first`. Supply the user outcome, exact affected flows/states, current/accepted source and target, settled choices, open decision, permitted edits and needed result. Request an operable comparison or focused design iteration using actual application components. Expect the proposed/accepted direction and exact approval scope, source/revision/artifact references, evidence, simulations/limits, unresolved questions and module/record implications. Reuse an adequate accepted design without another design round. Missing preview access leaves that review pending; continue independent planning.
4. **Resume planning after the specialist result.** Assess what its evidence establishes, incorporate the chosen/proposed flow, owners and affected contracts, and retain unresolved feasibility or human decisions explicitly. Specialist completion does not finish the plan or approve implementation. If a required human decision blocks later work, make it a visible boundary; do not hide it in dependent implementation tasks. Preserve existing approval without reopening settled choices.
5. Record the proportionate plan in its existing owner, using the [slice template](../assets/slice-template.md) only when useful. Include acceptance/completion conditions, exact focused checks and their claims, mandatory gates, integration ownership, remaining uncertainty and necessary record updates. Preserve consequential non-code rationale through the owning record or `write-adr` when relevant. Verify that links identify the exact design/revision and actual decision state rather than a chat summary.

**Deliver:** executable scoped plan or a concrete proposal with precise pending decisions, evidence/limits and next implications. Planning alone creates no tracker records or implementation. Continue to [task creation](create-tasks.md) when requested or [execution](execute.md) when already authorized; no extra planning approval is implied by the transition.
